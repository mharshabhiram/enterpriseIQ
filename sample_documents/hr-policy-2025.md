# Northwind Group — HR Policy 2025

## 1. Annual Leave
All full-time employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at 1.5 days per month. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year and must be used by 31 March.

## 2. Parental Leave
Employees with at least 12 months of continuous service are eligible for 12 weeks of paid parental leave at full salary. Partners are entitled to 2 weeks of paid leave.

## 3. Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Remote work requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least one week in advance. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time.

## 4. Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses must be submitted within 60 days with itemised receipts. Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to $50 per day. Purchases over $2,000 require director approval.

## 5. Learning & Development
Each employee has an annual learning budget of $800 for courses, books and conferences, subject to manager approval.

## 6. Responsibilities
Managers are responsible for approving leave within 5 working days. HR is responsible for maintaining leave records and answering policy questions at hr@northwind.example.
