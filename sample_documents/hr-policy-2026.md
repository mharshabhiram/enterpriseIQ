# Northwind Group — HR Policy 2026

## 1. Annual Leave
All full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at 1.67 days per month. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year and must be used by 30 June.

## 2. Parental Leave
Employees with at least 6 months of continuous service are eligible for 16 weeks of paid parental leave at full salary. Partners are entitled to 4 weeks of paid leave. A phased return of up to 4 weeks at 80% hours is available.

## 3. Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Manager approval is only required for fully remote arrangements. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time. A one-time home-office allowance of $400 is available.

## 4. Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days with itemised receipts. Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to $60 per day. Purchases over $5,000 require director approval.

## 5. Wellbeing
Employees receive 3 wellbeing days per year in addition to annual leave, and access to a free confidential counselling service.

## 6. Responsibilities
Managers are responsible for approving leave within 3 working days. HR is responsible for maintaining leave records and answering policy questions at people@northwind.example.
